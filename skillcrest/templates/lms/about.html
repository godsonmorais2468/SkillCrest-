About SkillCrest

SkillCrest is a full-stack Learning Management System developed using Python Django as an academic and portfolio project. It focuses on delivering IT-related courses through a structured and user-friendly online platform.

The system supports multiple user roles such as students and trainers, allowing learners to enroll in courses, access content, and track their learning progress efficiently.

Key Features
  • ✔ Role-based login (Student & Trainer)
  • ✔ Secure authentication & OTP verification
  • ✔ Course enrollment & progress tracking
  • ✔ Online payments integration

Our Mission

To design a secure and scalable e-learning platform that simplifies online education using modern web technologies.

Our Vision

To help students and freshers enhance their technical skills and prepare for real-world IT careers.
